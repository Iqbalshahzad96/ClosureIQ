# Accrual and Expense Recognition Policy

> **Notice**: Synthetic Demo Policy — For simulation and demonstration purposes only. This is not authoritative accounting advice or an internal policy of Enquest.

## Policy Metadata
- Policy ID: ACC-002
- Category: ACCRUAL
- Scope: Operating Expenses, Vendor Obligations, and Unbilled Invoices

## Section 1: Accrual Recognition Principle
1. Operating expenses incurred during the accounting fiscal period must be recognized in that period, regardless of whether a vendor invoice has been received.
2. Unbilled goods received notes (GRN) and completed service milestone deliveries must have an accrual journal entry prepared at period end.
3. Recurring monthly expenses (e.g., software licenses, utility provisions) must be accrued based on prior 3-month historical averages if current actuals are unavailable.

## Section 2: Materiality and Variance Tolerance
1. Any accrual variance exceeding 10% or 10,000.00 KES against the established baseline requires documented variance analysis and review.
2. Prepaid expenses spanning multiple periods must be amortized monthly on a straight-line basis over the benefit period.
3. Accrual reversals must occur on the first business day of the subsequent fiscal period to prevent duplicate expense recognition.
