# Accounts Payable Invoice Review Standard

> **Notice**: Synthetic Demo Policy — For simulation and demonstration purposes only. This is not authoritative accounting advice or an internal policy of Enquest.

## Policy Metadata
- Policy ID: ACC-004
- Category: ACCOUNTS_PAYABLE
- Scope: Vendor Invoices, Purchase Orders, and Payment Authorizations

## Section 1: Three-Way Matching Verification
1. Every accounts payable invoice must undergo automated 3-way matching against the approved purchase order and receiving goods receipt notes.
2. Invoices matching within unit price tolerance (up to 1%) and quantity delivered are approved for standard payment processing.
3. Invoices lacking a corresponding approved purchase order or exceeding purchase order quantities require accounts payable supervisor sign-off.

## Section 2: Duplicate Prevention and Overdue Aging
1. Duplicate invoice checking verifies vendor code, invoice number, amount, and invoice date across current and prior periods.
2. Invoices with exact matching vendor code and invoice number are quarantined immediately as duplicate exceptions.
3. Accounts payable balances aging beyond standard payment terms (30/60/90 days) require monthly aging review and vendor balance confirmation.
