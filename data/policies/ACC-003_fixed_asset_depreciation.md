# Fixed-Asset Depreciation Policy

> **Notice**: Synthetic Demo Policy — For simulation and demonstration purposes only. This is not authoritative accounting advice or an internal policy of Enquest.

## Policy Metadata
- Policy ID: ACC-003
- Category: DEPRECIATION
- Scope: Property, Plant, Equipment, and Intangibles

## Section 1: Capitalization and Depreciation Schedule
1. Tangible assets with a purchase cost equal to or exceeding 50,000.00 KES and an expected useful life greater than 12 months must be capitalized.
2. Depreciation is calculated monthly on a straight-line basis commencing in the month of asset acquisition and placement into service.
3. Standard useful life guidelines:
   - Computer hardware and IT equipment: 36 months (3 years)
   - Office furniture and fixtures: 60 months (5 years)
   - Motor vehicles and transport equipment: 60 months (5 years)
   - Heavy machinery and plant facilities: 120 months (10 years)

## Section 2: Depreciation Audit and Anomaly Detection
1. Monthly recorded depreciation must match the formula: (Acquisition Cost - Salvage Value) / Useful Life in Months.
2. A variance greater than 1.00 KES between posted depreciation and the scheduled depreciation table triggers an audit exception.
3. Fully depreciated assets must remain on the fixed asset register at nominal salvage value until official disposal or write-off.
